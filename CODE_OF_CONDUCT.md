# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want WMLST to be a project that a first-year PhD student, a hospital microbiologist
who has never used a terminal, and a twenty-year bioinformatics veteran can all take
part in without being made to feel small.

Everyone involved — maintainers, contributors, people filing issues — pledges to make
participation a harassment-free experience for all, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Things that make this project work:

- Being kind, patient, and welcoming — especially to people asking a question that has
  an obvious answer. It was not obvious to them.
- Assuming good faith and asking before accusing.
- Giving and accepting constructive criticism gracefully. Criticise the code, never the
  person who wrote it.
- Saying "I don't know" and "I was wrong" out loud.
- Focusing on what is best for the people who use this software.

Things that are not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Condescension about someone's operating system, editor, language, institution or
  level of experience. Contempt for Windows users is part of why this project exists;
  it is not welcome in it.
- Anything else a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull requests,
discussions — and whenever an individual is officially representing the project in
public.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer:

**Giovanni Lorenzin, IOWA-BioTech** — open a private security advisory on
<https://github.com/iowa69/WMLST> or contact the maintainer directly through the
address listed in `CITATION.cff`.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The maintainer is
obliged to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

Consequences, in the usual escalation: a private written warning; a public warning; a
temporary ban from interaction with the project; a permanent ban. Maintainers who do not
follow or enforce this code in good faith may face temporary or permanent repercussions
themselves.
